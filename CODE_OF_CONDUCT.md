# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Treat people with dignity, assume good intent while remaining open to correction, and give constructive feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing private information without consent, and disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical; retaliation is unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an available private contact method. Do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue. If no private contact is available, ask a maintainer in a discussion how to reach them privately without describing the incident.
